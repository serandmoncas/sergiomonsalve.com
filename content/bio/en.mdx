My name is Sergio Monsalve. I'm 40, a systems engineer, professor and AI engineer. I live in Guarne, Antioquia, in a house shared by computers, mushroom crops, a guitar and my cat Appa.

I've spent more than 13 years building software and more than 20 connected to Universidad EAFIT, first as a student and then guiding other students. If I had to sum myself up, I'd say I learn by doing, and I can't keep what I learn without sharing it.

There's a line in my notes that guides me more than I'd like to admit: *"This shit has to be fun"*. If I'm not enjoying it, I'm doing something wrong.

## Where I come from

I was born in Medellín, at Hospital San Vicente de Paúl. I lived in Medellín and in Santa Elena, and in September 2026 I moved to Guarne, where my crops and building projects are today.

Every morning my routine starts with a section I call "Love": my family and friends. First on that list is my mom, Angela María Castañeda.

## My path

My path hasn't been a straight line. I went from analyzing data to leading teams, from co-founding a company to building AI agents, and I always came back to the same thing: teaching.

I started studying Systems Engineering at EAFIT in 2004. A year later, in 2005, I began leading research groups (*semilleros*), because nobody else wanted to. I stayed with them until I graduated in 2013. That's where I found out I loved helping others learn as much as learning myself. Today I'm an MSc in Engineering candidate, with a thesis on how to teach programming in the age of AI.

## What has tested me

The last few years have tested me. In 2023 an important relationship ended, I crashed my motorcycle, broke my right hand and went through surgery; months later I split my head open. In 2024 came a second arm surgery, and my aunt Marta died.

On April 8, 2025, I was let go from Globant. A month later I started something I called *Selfsearch* in my notes: a search for who I am when I no longer have a job title to define me.

I didn't choose any of it, but each one forced me to ask what I wanted to build with the time I have. Almost everything I do today comes out of that search.

## What makes me happy

Almost everything I love is done with my hands, and many of those passions end up becoming projects.

- **Guitar.** I take lessons and log every practice. I play Latin American music: *Romance anónimo*, Chalo Álvarez, Briela Ojeda, Edson Velandia, Los Jaivas and Pedro Pastor.
- **Mushrooms and plants.** I grow oyster and shiitake mushrooms, dehydrate them and design automated capsules to grow them. I'm also interested in medicinal cannabis and *Psilocybe cubensis*.
- **Natural building.** Bamboo (guadua), superadobe, geodesic domes, carpentry and resins.
- **Outdoors.** Bushcraft and camping: shelters, fire, knots, navigation and birdwatching.
- **Movement.** [Paragliding](/en/blog/2026-05-09-mas-alla-del-codigo), cycling, skating and walking.
- **Cooking.** Smoking, preserves and barbecue.
- **Electronics and "toys".** IoT, drones, RC planes, 3D printing and solar power. As I once wrote: we don't grow up, we just want more expensive toys.
- **Reading.** Stoicism, happiness and entrepreneurship: *Daily Stoic*, *Authentic Happiness*, *Educated* and *Zero to One*.
- **My journal.** I write every day using a template: Focus, Love, Health, Money, To-dos and Closing.

## How I think and work

I believe you learn by doing, practicing the hard parts and adjusting as you go. That idea runs through my method, my classes and the way I build software.

- **Experiential education.** I'm inspired by constructivism and constructionism: teaching with real projects, not just theory.
- **Do it myself.** I'd rather build my own tools, from a growing capsule to my own journal app.
- **Discipline with method.** I work with specs, plans and acceptance criteria before writing code.
- **Pay it forward.** Time, knowledge and passions in service of others.
- **Designing life.** I use a compass and a timeline to look at my past and plan my next 20 years.
- **Enjoy it.** If it isn't fun, I'm not doing it right.
